09 — Usage

Do & Don't

Keep the mark confident and uncluttered so it reads like a global technology company.

✓ Do
  • Give the logo generous clear space (≥ the height of the symbol).
  • Use navy or white backgrounds for maximum contrast.
  • Use the symbol alone for app icons and avatars.
  • Keep “Go” navy and “Via” green.
✕ Don't
  • Don't add trucks, vans, boxes or location pins.
  • Don't stretch, rotate or recolour the mark.
  • Don't place green text on navy without enough contrast.
  • Don't add drop shadows or outlines to the wordmark.
